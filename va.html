RMC is a Board for Contractors specialty on Class A, B, or C contractor licenses. Names, license numbers, addresses, expiration dates, and specialty codes are transcribed from the official DPOR ASCII tab-delimited regulant lists (2705 A / B / C) — nothing invented. DPOR does not publish phone numbers on these lists, so phone is marked unknown. Address of record includes out-of-state firms as printed. Status changes; re-check DPOR License Lookup before hiring.

Virginia Code also requires professional radon testers and mitigators to hold current NRPP or NRSB certification (VDH Indoor Radon Program). This page is the state contractor RMC roster only — not a measurement-professional list. Search NRPP/NRSB for testers, and verify both RMC (when hiring a mitigation contractor) and national certification.

This page covers indoor air only. Discounted VDH short-term kits (while supplies last): vdhradon.org.

Always verify the contractor license and national certification are still current before hiring. This index does not rank contractors or take referral fees.
