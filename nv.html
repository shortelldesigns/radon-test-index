Nevada radon measurement and mitigation professionals (air)

25 certified measurement professionals and 7 certified mitigation contractors from the official UNR Extension Nevada Radon Education Program lists. Updated / retrieved 2026-09-26 (US/Pacific).

If you are not sure you need a pro yet, see when a DIY radon test kit is enough or how to test a home for radon.

Nevada does not appear to issue a separate state radon license roster. The EPA-funded Nevada Radon Education Program (NREP) at University of Nevada, Reno Extension compiles certified measurement and mitigation professionals from the national voluntary proficiency programs NRPP and NRSB, and lists a Nevada Contractors License number when provided for mitigators. NREP states it does not endorse or recommend any company or individual over another. Names, certification IDs, expirations, companies, areas, phones, and emails/websites below are transcribed from the official NREP tables on 2026-09-26 (US/Pacific). Nothing invented. Missing fields are shown as —.

Odd spellings are kept as printed (for example, Sebastion Tumbale). Some people appear in both Northern Nevada and Southern Nevada tables — both region rows are kept as published; they are not merged. Re-check NRPP or NRSB credentials and, for mitigation work, the Nevada State Contractors Board before hiring.

This page covers indoor air only, not well water. Program contact as printed: Program Director's Office, 4955 Energy Way, Reno, NV; chrisk@unr.edu; (775) 336-0254.

Always verify a professional is still listed and that credentials are current before hiring. This index does not rank contractors or take referral fees. No Amazon kit links on this state page.
