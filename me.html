Maine registers radon service providers through the Radiation Control Program (DHHS / Division of Environmental Health). Names, license numbers, addresses, phones, expiration dates, and air authorities are transcribed from official ALMS “Search active licenses only” CSV downloads for Test Air, Portable Lab (air), and Mitigate Air — nothing invented. Status changes; re-check ALMS before hiring.

Under Maine law, when a residence is for sale, testing and mitigation must be done by a registered radon service provider (owner/occupant may not perform those services for a sale). This page covers indoor air only. Water authorities (Test Water / Mitigate Water / Stationary Lab water) are not used for the service column here; re-check ALMS if you need water radon services. Stationary Lab (air) analytical labs are also listed in ALMS and are not separately merged onto this table unless they also hold Test Air, Portable Lab (air), or Mitigate Air.

Address of record includes 103 Maine and 18 out-of-state companies as published.

Always verify registration is still active in ALMS before hiring. This index does not rank contractors or take referral fees.
