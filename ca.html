California Department of Public Health — Indoor Radon Program

California radon testers and mitigators (air)

231 certified testers and 12 certified mitigators from the official CDPH Certified Radon Services Providers page. Updated / retrieved 2026-09-24 (US/Pacific).

If you are not sure you need a pro yet, see when a DIY radon test kit is enough or how to test a home for radon.

California law requires radon service providers to hold NRPP or NRSB approval and to appear on the CDPH certified list before offering radon measurement or mitigation to the public (California Health and Safety Code §§ 106750–106795). Names, certification numbers, expiration dates, state of record, phones, single-family testing flags, and California contractor license numbers below are transcribed from the official CDPH tables on 2026-09-24 (US/Pacific). Nothing invented.

CDPH states these lists are not updated daily. To verify a provider not listed here, email radonprogram@cdph.ca.gov or call 916-449-5674 / 800-745-7236. Mitigators must also hold a valid California Contractors State License Board (CSLB) license in an allowed category (B, C20, C36, or D64) — re-check the CSLB number before hiring.

This page covers indoor air only, not well water. Out-of-state addresses of record appear as printed on the CDPH tester table. CDPH also hosts a separate Laboratories PDF (header dated 2/21/17 on the PDF itself) — linked under Official sources; that PDF is not re-tabulated here because of its age.

The official tester table prints some certification numbers more than once (duplicate rows as published). This index keeps every row as printed.

Always verify a company is still certified and credentialed before hiring. This index does not rank contractors or take referral fees. No Amazon kit links on this state page.
