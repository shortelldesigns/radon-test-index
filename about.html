About Radon Test Index

Radon Test Index is an independent public directory compiled by Stephen Shortell. It lists people who already appear on official state certification lists and on EPA-recognized national proficiency listings. It is a reading of those records, not a new credential.

What this is not

  • Not a laboratory and not a radon testing or mitigation company.
  • Not Shortell Designs, and not a product or service of any design studio.
  • Not endorsed by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, NRPP, NRSB, or any state radon office.
  • Not a ranking, marketplace, or referral desk. There is no paid placement and there are no affiliate links.

Verify before you hire

Licenses expire and lists go stale. Before you hire anyone, confirm a current credential with the issuing program:

  • NRPP Find a Pro (EPA-recognized national proficiency)
  • NRSB (EPA-recognized national proficiency)
  • Iowa: HHS radon program and AMANDA license search
  • Pennsylvania: DEP Radon Services Directory
  • Ohio: ODH hire a professional (an NRPP/NRSB listing is not an Ohio license)
  • Minnesota: MDH licensed service providers
  • North Dakota: DEQ personnel list (PDF)
  • Colorado: DORA Office of Radon Professionals and license lookup
  • Illinois: IEMA-OHS active licensee listing
  • New York: NYS DOH certified testers and mitigation contractors
  • Kansas: KDHE radon professionals and certified technician list (PDF, 2026-07-14)
  • Nebraska: DHHS radon program licensed measurement and mitigation business PDFs
  • Indiana: IPLA license verification (Profession: Radon, Status: Active) and IDOH Lead & Healthy Homes radon contractors
  • Montana: DEQ Radon Control Program and Radon Service Providers map (no state license; DEQ convenience list of NRPP/NRSB providers)

EPA consumer page on finding a qualified provider: How can you find a qualified radon service provider in your area?

Where the names come from

Retrieved 2026-08-29 unless noted. Iowa (265) from HHS certified measurement and credentialed mitigation specialist PDFs generated 2026-07-16. Pennsylvania (724) from DEP tester and mitigator directories dated 2026-08-03; the DEP demo account was omitted. Ohio (8) from individual NRPP listings that publish an Ohio location. Ohio official licensee list is only available as a JavaScript map; this index does not invent names to fill that gap. Minnesota (367) from the MDH statewide licensed service-provider search. North Dakota (44) from the DEQ personnel PDF updated 2025-09-09. Colorado (668) from DORA active RME and RMT licenses in the Colorado Information Marketplace. Illinois (596) from the IEMA-OHS Radon Active Licensee Listing. New York (95) from the NYS DOH certified testers and mitigation contractors pages revised May 2026 (NYS does not issue a state radon license). Kansas (237) from the KDHE certified measurement and mitigation technician PDF dated 2026-07-14 (retrieved 2026-09-01). Nebraska (114) from DHHS licensed measurement (2026-07-28) and mitigation (2026-05-21) business PDFs (retrieved 2026-09-01). Indiana (321) from the IPLA public Active Radon license search (Primary/Secondary Testers and Mitigators; retrieved 2026-09-02). Montana (32) from the DEQ Radon Service Providers GIS FeatureServer table (retrieved 2026-09-03); Montana has no state radon license — DEQ publishes a mitigation-oriented convenience list of nationally certified providers. South Dakota has no state license and no official roster; the South Dakota page is an explainer that links DANR, NRPP, and NRSB only (retrieved 2026-09-04). Wisconsin has the same gap and is not listed yet.

People who appear on both a measurement list and a mitigation list are marked as both. Phone numbers from the source lists are not republished here.
