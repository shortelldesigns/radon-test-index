Oregon radon measurement and mitigation companies (air)

36 measurement companies and 12 mitigation companies from the official OHA certified-company pages. Updated / retrieved 2026-09-25 (US/Pacific).

If you are not sure you need a pro yet, see when a DIY radon test kit is enough or how to test a home for radon.

OHA publishes separate public lists of radon measurement and mitigation companies located in Oregon that have at least one technician on staff certified by NRPP or NRSB. OHA states these lists are for informational purposes only and are not an endorsement. Names, contacts, phones, websites, and cities below are transcribed from the official OHA tables on 2026-09-25 (US/Pacific). Nothing invented. Missing phones are shown as —.

Company names are kept as printed on each OHA table (including spelling differences between the measurement and mitigation lists). A company may appear on one list, both, or neither over time — re-check the OHA pages and the technician’s NRPP or NRSB credential before hiring. OHA notes that organizations outside Oregon can also provide testing devices or services.

This page covers indoor air only, not well water. Oregon does not issue a separate state radon license on these pages; listing reflects OHA’s NRPP/NRSB staff-certification criteria as published.

Always verify a company is still listed and that staff credentials are current before hiring. This index does not rank contractors or take referral fees. No Amazon kit links on this state page.
