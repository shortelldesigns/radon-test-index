Indiana licenses individual Primary Radon Testers, Secondary Radon Testers, and Radon Mitigators through IPLA / the Indiana Department of Health Lead & Healthy Homes Division. Names, license numbers, license types, and city/state of record are transcribed from the public IPLA EVerification Active Radon search. People who hold more than one Active radon license are merged into one row. City is the address of record; some licensees are based in a neighboring state. Credential shows each Active Indiana license number and type (RTP primary tester, RTS secondary tester, RTM mitigator).

Always verify the credential is still current with the IPLA license search (Profession: Radon, Status: Active) before hiring. This index does not rank contractors or take referral fees.
