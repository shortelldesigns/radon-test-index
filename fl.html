Florida radon mitigation businesses (air)

27 certified mitigation businesses from the official Florida DOH PDF (report dated 31-Aug-26). Retrieved 2026-09-16 (US/Pacific).

If you are not sure you need a pro yet, see when a DIY radon test kit is enough or how to test a home for radon.

Florida requires DOH certification for businesses and individuals offering radon mitigation (and measurement). Names, addresses, phones, service areas, and expiration dates are transcribed from the official public mitigation-business PDF — nothing invented. DOH notes the list may include recently expired certifications that are renewing; contact the Radon Program to verify status before hiring.

This page is the mitigation business roster only. Certified measurement/testing businesses are on a separate DOH PDF (linked below) — not transcribed here tonight. Individuals (specialists/technicians) also appear on separate DOH lists. This page covers indoor air only.

Always verify certification is still current with the Florida Department of Health before hiring. This index does not rank contractors or take referral fees.
