This table is only Active licensees who opted in to the Construction Business Registry (CBR) under classification S354 – Radon Mitigation. DOPL states that only Active licensees who opted in appear in CBR search results — so this list is incomplete versus every licensed mitigator in Utah. Names, license numbers, and city/state are transcribed from the official CBR search for Contractor + S354 on 2026-09-20 (US/Pacific). Nothing invented. No phones (CBR list view does not publish phone on the search results table used here).

Utah requires radon mitigation system work to be performed by a licensed contractor, and mitigation professionals must hold NRPP (or equivalent national) certification as defined in Utah Administrative Code R156-55a-302b. Prefer NRPP- or NRSB-certified pros, then re-check the DOPL license and national credential before hiring.

This page covers indoor air only, not well water. One out-of-state city of record (Grand Junction, CO) appears as printed on the CBR.

Always verify a company is still Active and credentialed before hiring. This index does not rank contractors or take referral fees. No Amazon kit links on this state page.
