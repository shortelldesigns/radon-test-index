About this index

Compiled by Stephen Shortell. Not a lab. Not EPA-endorsed. Always verify credentials with the issuing agency.

How to use the lists

  • State certification or licensure is what lets someone test or mitigate for others in that state. National NRPP/NRSB credentials are not a substitute.
  • People who appear on both a tester list and a mitigator list are marked measurement and mitigation.
  • City is the address of record on the source list. Some Iowa and Pennsylvania licensees are based in neighboring states.
  • Ohio’s official list is map-only. This index ships the 8 names that could be verified from NRPP and points you to NRPP, NRSB, and ODH for everyone else.
