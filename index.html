About this index

Compiled by Stephen Shortell. Not a lab. Not EPA-endorsed. Always verify credentials with the issuing agency.

How to use the lists

  • State certification or licensure is what lets someone test or mitigate for others in that state. National NRPP/NRSB credentials are not a substitute where a state license is required.
  • People who appear on both a tester list and a mitigator list are marked measurement and mitigation.
  • City is the address of record on the source list. Some licensees are based in a neighboring state.
  • Ohio official list is map-only. This index ships the 8 names that could be verified from NRPP and points you to NRPP, NRSB, and ODH for everyone else.
  • Wisconsin and South Dakota do not publish a state licensee list (no state license). Use NRPP or NRSB. This index does not invent names to fill that gap.
  • New York does not issue a state radon license. The New York page is the complete NYS DOH public list of nationally certified testers and mitigators (revised May 2026).
